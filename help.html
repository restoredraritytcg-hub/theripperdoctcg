1. What you need

  • The intake page — intake.html on your site, opened with your PIN. The Scan Station is its Scan tab.
  • One of these to capture cards:
    • a flatbed scanner connected to a Windows PC or Mac (best quality — e.g. a Canon CanoScan LiDE 400), or
    • a phone camera (quick, no extra equipment).
  • Chrome or Microsoft Edge on the computer if you want the Scan Station to pick up new scans by itself (“Watch scan folder”). Other browsers can still use Choose files or drag and drop.
  • A dark, plain background — a black sheet of paper or card stock behind the cards. It lets the station find the card edges.
Scans are saved to the same Cloudinary account your intake photos use, and the details go into the Scans column (column V) of the Items tab in your Google Sheet.

2. Setting up a flatbed scanner

Any flatbed scanner works if it can save JPEG (or PNG) files into a folder. These are the settings that matter:

SettingUseWhy
Resolution1200 dpi for one card · 600 dpi for 2–3 cards at onceSharp close-ups of corners and edges. The station reads the dpi from the file.
Color modeColor
File typeJPEG (high quality)PNG works too but files are 5–10× bigger.
Output size / scale100%Anything else (e.g. 105%) throws off the dpi and the centering.
Sharpening (“Unsharp mask”)OffIt adds halos around the border lines.
Scan areaAbout 3 × 4 in around one card; about 8.5 × 4.2 in for three in a rowScanning the whole glass makes huge files.
Save toOne folder, e.g. Card ScansThat's the folder the Scan Station watches.

Canon CanoScan LiDE 400 on Windows

  1. Install IJ Scan Utility from Canon's site and open it. (Its simple “Custom” scan stops at 600 dpi.)
  2. For 1200 dpi click ScanGear (the “Driver” button). Switch to Advanced Mode.
  3. Set Color, 1200 dpi (or 600 for several cards), Output Size 100%, Unsharp Mask off.
  4. In Preferences → Preview, set Cropping Frame on Previewed Images to Display Last Frame — otherwise it re-crops tightly around the card on every preview.
  5. Use the whole-glass view (not thumbnail view). Put the card in the top-left corner of the glass, preview, and draw the frame about 3 × 4 in around it. ScanGear keeps that frame for the next scans.
  6. In IJ Scan Utility's Settings → Driver Settings, pick the save folder (e.g. Card Scans) and JPEG.
  7. Lay a black sheet over the card before closing the lid (the lid itself is white).

On a Mac

Use Image Capture (built in) or Canon's IJ Scan Utility for Mac: choose your scanner, Show Details, set Kind = Color, Resolution = 1200 (or 600), Scale = 100%, Format = JPEG, and “Scan To” your scan folder. Draw the scan area around the card the same way.

Several cards in one scan: place up to 3 cards in a row, pushed to the top edge of the glass with a little space between them, at 600 dpi. Set Cards per scan in the Scan Station to match. Cards are matched to card numbers left to right. When you flip them for the backs, keep them in the same order.

3. Using the Scan Station

  1. Open the intake page and tap Scan — or on any card, tap 📷 Scan before / in progress / after, which opens the station on that card.
  2. Pick the Order, the Card to start with, the Stage (Before, In progress, After) and the Side. The blue line shows exactly where the next scan will go.
  3. Add scans one of three ways:
    • Watch scan folder (Chrome/Edge on a computer): choose the folder your scanner saves to. Every new scan saved there is picked up within a few seconds — files already in the folder are ignored. Keep the page open while you scan.
    • Choose files or drag files onto the page — they're added in name order.
    • Use camera on a phone — see the next section.
  4. Scan the front, then flip the card and scan the back. After the back, the station moves to the next card on its own.
  5. Each scan is straightened, cropped, measured and saved automatically. Check the result in the list below; use Fix centering or Undo if needed.
Scanning the same card, stage and side again replaces the earlier scan. The station tells you when that will happen.

4. Scanning with a phone camera

  1. Open the intake page on the phone, go to Scan, pick the order and card, then tap Use camera (allow camera access).
  2. Lay the card on a dark, plain surface in even light. No flash — it causes glare.
  3. Hold the phone flat above the card so it fills the outline. On most phones a bubble level appears; when it turns blue and steady, the photo is taken automatically. You can always tap the round button instead. (iPhone: tap Turn on level and allow motion access.)
  4. Flip the card for the back; then put the next card down. Tap Done when finished.

Tilted photos are flattened automatically. A flatbed scan is still more accurate for centering and much sharper for corner close-ups.

5. Centering, close-ups and grade limits

  • Centering is measured on every border, half an inch from each corner, and shown as L/R and T/B — e.g. 55/45.
  • ⚠ Check the centering markers means the two measurements on a side disagree, or the border isn't a solid color (full-art cards). Tap Fix centering (in the station, or Centering on the card in an order).
  • In the centering tool, the gold marker is the card's outer edge and the cyan marker is the inside edge of the border. Drag them, zoom with the + / − buttons, then Save. Reset puts them back where they were measured.
  • Close-ups of the 4 corners and 4 edges are made from each scan automatically — tap a scan to flip through them.
  • Centering allows shows the best PSA, CGC and BGS grade the centering alone would allow (it uses the back too once it's scanned). Only you see this.

6. What your customers see

Your customer's link (the Customer link on each order, or Track Your Submission on your website) shows, for each card: the before / in-progress / after scans, front and back, the L/R and T/B centering, the corner and edge close-ups, and the photos on the order. They don't see the “Centering allows” grade limits, the ⚠ check warnings or any buttons to change things.

7. Installing the intake page as an app

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open theripperdoctcg.com/intake.html in Chrome and enter your PIN (tick Remember me on this phone).
  2. Tap ⋮ (top right) → Install app (or Add to Home screen → Install).
  3. The RipperDoc icon appears on the home screen and opens full-screen. Samsung Internet: ☰ → Add page to → Home screen.

iPhone / iPad (Safari)

  1. Open theripperdoctcg.com/intake.html in Safari and enter your PIN.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow) → Add to Home Screen → Add.

Windows or Mac (Chrome / Edge)

  1. Open the intake page, then click the install icon at the right end of the address bar (or ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app; Edge: … → Apps → Install this site as an app).
  2. It opens in its own window — handy to leave the Scan Station watching your scan folder.
The app updates itself: when your site is updated, the next time you open the app you get the new version. If something looks old, close the app completely and open it again.

8. Troubleshooting FAQ

I don't see a “Watch scan folder” button

Folder watching needs Chrome or Edge on a computer. Safari, Firefox and phones don't allow it. Use Choose files or drag the scans onto the page instead.

I scanned but nothing showed up in the station
  • Make sure the line under “Add scans” says Watching … with the right folder name, and the page is still open.
  • Check the scanner really saved to that folder (open the folder and look for the new file).
  • Only files saved after you started watching are picked up. For an older file, use Choose files.
  • After a computer restart, click Watch scan folder again and allow access.
The Google Drive folder can't be chosen on a Mac

Chrome on a Mac may not open folders inside Google Drive. Make a normal folder (e.g. in Documents) for the scanner and the station, and add it to Google Drive's backup if you want copies there.

“No card edge found — saved as the whole picture”

The station couldn't see where the card ends, so it saved the picture as it is (no close-ups or centering). Use a darker background with space around the card, check the card isn't touching the edge of the scan area, and scan again — the new scan replaces the old one.

The centering numbers look wrong
  • Check the scanner's Output size is 100%.
  • Open Fix centering and drag the markers onto the real edges, then Save.
  • Full-art and borderless cards have no border to measure — set the markers by eye or ignore the numbers.
Two or three cards in one scan went to the wrong card numbers

Cards are read left to right and matched to the card numbers starting at the card shown in the blue line. Use Undo on the wrong ones, pick the right starting card, and scan again. Keep the backs in the same order as the fronts.

How do I remove a scan?

In the station, Undo. On an order, open the card and tap Remove under the scan (tap twice to confirm). This removes it from the order and the customer link; the picture itself stays in your Cloudinary account (you can delete it there if you need the space).

How much storage do scans use?

Each saved side is about 1–3 MB on Cloudinary, so a card with before and after, front and back is roughly 5–10 MB. Cloudinary's free plan gives 25 credits a month — one credit is about 1 GB of storage, 1 GB of viewing, or 1,000 close-up crops (each crop is made once, then reused). A few hundred cards fit comfortably; check Cloudinary → Dashboard for your usage.

The camera won't open on my phone

Allow camera access when asked. If you said no before: Android Chrome → 🔒 next to the address → Permissions → Camera → Allow. iPhone → Settings → Safari → Camera → Allow. Inside the installed app it uses the same setting as the browser.

A scan says “Not saved”

Usually the internet dropped while uploading. Tap Try again on that scan. If it keeps failing, check your Cloudinary upload preset is still set to Unsigned.

Scans don't show on the customer's link

Your Google Apps Script needs the version with scan support. In the Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script, paste the latest Code.gs (keep your PIN line), Save, then Deploy → Manage deployments → ✏️ → New version → Deploy.
